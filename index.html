himalaya OAuth

A personal OAuth 2.0 client for Gmail, used by the himalaya command-line email client.

What it does

This OAuth client lets a single person — its owner — sign in to their own Gmail account and read, search, and send email from the himalaya CLI on their own computer. It requests only the https://mail.google.com/ scope.

There is no server, no multi-user service, and no public sign-up. Tokens are stored locally on the owner's machine with file permissions restricted to that user.
